## What changed

- `FeatureCadence.MARKET` (new) — timeframe-independent like TICK, fed from
  the per-instrument market snapshot via `FeatureComputer.onMarket(MarketView)`.
- `FeatureRegistry`: 42 append-only lines, ids 3..44 in `CandleLiveColumns`
  transport order — the 12 stats then the attribute-major 30-value depth
  ladder. Names pinned in `feature-registry-pins.tsv`.
- `MarketValueComputer` (new): one extractor + one presence gate per field;
  a never-seen group/level holds NaN and is skipped by the writer.
- `PerInstrumentFeatures`: market state + `onMarket(view)`;
  `latest`/`latestLive`/`snapshot` treat MARKET ids as timeframe-independent.
- `StrategyHostFunction`: `updateFeaturesOnMarket(slot)` after every successful
  `decodeMarketSnapshot` (canonical forming row and `TF=MKT` row) — before the
  strategy fan-out and before any sealed row of a closing window is built.
- No DDL, sink, flag or operator change: the `features MAP<INT,DOUBLE>` already
  exists in `candle_features`.

## Why

The operator asked for the full market/book columns to be visible in the one
multi-TF feature table: each candle row shows its aggregate plus the feature
values, including all 30 depth values. The live view (CHG-501/502) made them
readable; this change makes them stored, so any reader can see the book at each
candle's close without scanning the raw tick table.

  presence follows the capture gates (`hasStats`; a depth trio gates on its
  side's price at that level), and `0` inside a present group keeps the
  MarketView "not provided" convention.

  `make day ARGS="start"`, or a rollout now) is needed before sealed rows carry
  the new ids. Unit + host-level tests cover the write path; the first stored
