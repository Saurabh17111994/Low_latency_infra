# CHG-553 — the append identity counters become observable (OQ9)

- **Status:** implemented (additive observability; no append-path logic changed).
- **Filed:** 2026-10-06
- **Source:** OQ9 of `docs/plans/2026-10-06-p99-integrated-program.md`. `append.pending.records`
  reads ~500 while the same run's own numbers say it must be ~0: `append.latency.ms` count ==
  `tick.throughput` == 485 789 on all three fleet JVMs (i.e. every accepted record completed), zero
  retry/uncertain/fatal lines, and Little's law on the same gauge (512 at 1 620 records/s ≈ 316 ms)
  contradicts the measured accept→ack mean of 9.8–21.5 ms by ~32×. The gauge cannot be checked
  against anything today because `AppendTracker`'s own cumulative counters are never exported, and
  sampling the same gauge faster cannot distinguish "records really are in flight" from "the gauge
  over-counts".

## What changed

1. **`OtlpMetricsEmitter`** gains four cumulative sums — `append.accepted.records`,
   `append.appended.records`, `append.failed.records`, `append.rejected.records` — plus one gauge,
   `append.pending.records.max` (per-window peak of the pending gauge, reset at each flush so a
   one-off spike cannot follow the run forever).
2. **`IngestionService`** feeds them from `tracker.totalAccepted()/totalAppended()/totalFailed()/
   totalRejected()` at the existing per-tick site (`metrics.setPendingRecords`), i.e. four
   `AtomicLong` reads per accepted tick — the accessors already existed and were previously read
   only in the uncertainty journal and the shutdown summary.

## Why this and not a faster pending sample

The counters make the tracker's own identity testable from an arm, with no reliance on timing:

```text
accepted - appended - failed == pending.records      (expected at every flush)
```

If it holds while pending stays ~500, those records genuinely have no completion yet (and the
latency histogram legitimately cannot see them); if it breaks, the gauge is over-counting and every
"residence time" derived from it is meaningless. A 1 s sample of the same gauge answers neither
question, which is why the approved "1 s pending sampling" step was replaced by this instrument.

## What deliberately did NOT change

* **No change to `AppendTracker`** — its accept gate, halt behaviour, warning thresholds and
  `forceDrain()` semantics are untouched; the counters were already maintained.
* **No change to the append path** — `RawTickWriter` and every completion branch are byte-identical.
* **No new always-on cost beyond five metric lines per 10 s flush** and four atomic reads per tick.
* **No `make gate` step.**
