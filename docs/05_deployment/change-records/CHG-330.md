## Why

"Hop by configuration" is only true if the switch cannot silently disappear or
become unsafe. The checks make the switch a contract (defaults fail-closed,
`fake` cannot reach Arrow, enablement stays signed, the daily runner stays
offline), and the runbook makes the procedure executable by an operator without
re-deriving it from the code.
