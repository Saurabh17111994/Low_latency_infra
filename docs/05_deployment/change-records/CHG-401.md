## Notes

- **Fail-closed registry:** `*_IMAGE`/`*_VERSION` map to `runtime.lock` today; any other claim
  variable fails with "add it to this test's registry or drop the claim", so a future claim cannot
  be silently unchecked.
- **Non-vacuous by construction:** the claim rule asserts at least two LIVE digest claims are
  checked — deleting the `Claim:` lines fails the guard instead of shrinking its coverage.
- **The guard is the ledger's second reader:** `env_facts.py check` runs each row's `Check:`
  line; this test covers the prose and the claims those lines do not.
