## Why the guard is a pre-step, not part of the drill

The gateway's `IntentReader` forwards pending rows once the gate is `ENABLED`; by the time the drill runs, forwarding has already happened. The guard therefore runs before the gateway switch (step 1) — the runbook places it at step 0. The daily runner (`make day`) is not a home: it refuses execution flags by design (D1) and must not be run during a drill window.
