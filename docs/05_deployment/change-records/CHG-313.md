## What changed

- `DrillFixtureHygieneTest` (common, `@Tag("integration")`): after the drills it lists every
  database/table and fails on any fixture-family survivor (databases `prewarm_`, `gateway_replay_`,
  `gateway_ledger_`, `gateway_page_`, `proj_`, `b4_halted_`, `quar_`; tables `compat_`, `chg100`,
  `wp3_gate_`, `rr_settle_`, `a2probe_`). Read-only: it lists, never drops.
- `tablet-orphan-sweep.py`: `--check` reports and exits 1; `--sweep` removes. Only directories
  empty at two scans ~3 s apart and absent from the live ZooKeeper catalog are removed, with
  `rmdir` (never recursive); dot-dirs and `remote-log-index-cache` are skipped.
- `gate_preflight.py` (step 1 guard): reads the tablet's effective `remote.data.dir` and
  `remote.log.task-interval-duration`; fails when a remote URI is live while tiering is off (the
  CHG-312 cost), and folds in the orphan-dir check plus the tablet-registry advisory.
- `make drill-live`: the hygiene guard runs in its own Maven invocation **after** the drill
  classes (the reactor would otherwise run common first), then the orphan sweep runs regardless of
  the drill result so a failing drill still cleans up.

## Why it is safe

- Every removal is double-verified: empty at two scans, and its database/table is absent from the
  live catalog; a mistaken match would be refused by `rmdir` for a non-empty dir anyway.
- The guard is read-only and fails closed; the preflight is read-only and fails closed. Neither
  can repair or delete anything.
- Offline runs self-skip (FLUSS_BOOTSTRAP unset), so `make test` and module suites are unaffected;
  the drill-list module-exclusion guards confirm common-module classes need no gate exclusion.
