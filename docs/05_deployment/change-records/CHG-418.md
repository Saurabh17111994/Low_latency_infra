## Notes

- **Why `Ok(None)` and not `Err`:** the live node treats `Err` from mass status as fatal
  ("Failed to get mass status from {client_id}" aborts startup), while `Ok(None)` is the upstream
  "adapter could not provide reports" path — it warns and continues (measured in
  `nautilus-live`'s startup reconciliation match). The plan's operator decision (N1) chose
  warn-and-skip over a hard boot refusal.
- **One tick, not three:** the override records exactly one `record_tick()` per round; `record_tick`
  also pumps the report stream, which is what books an intraday postback between shutdowns (M1-3).
  The soak's progress counter therefore advances once per round — its comment now says so; the
  assertion (non-decreasing per sample, advanced over the leg) is unchanged and passed.
- **The marker is the contract:** `MASS_STATUS_UNSUPPORTED` is the string a future durable fill
  source replaces; the test pins the marker, not the wording around it.
