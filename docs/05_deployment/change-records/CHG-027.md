## What landed and why

The user decision (2026-08-18) re-architects the execution half of the platform:

**Nautilus → go-arrow bridge → Arrow.** Nautilus (Rust-native trading engine) becomes the
execution/position core — OMS, position engine, risk engine, reconciliation, fill dedup, and the
event-store audit — replacing three parallel implementations (Action Capture's lifecycle/position
projectors, Babysitter observation, Executor's gate/attempt/audit machinery). The go-arrow bridge
(a localhost service wrapping the already-pinned `go-arrow` SDK) is the **only** component that
talks to Arrow. Custom glue remains: the Fluss trade-row reader, projection sinks to the
`Fills`/`Order_Lifecycle`/`Positions`/`Execution_*` tables, the two-person gate + fencing, and
attempt/correlation mapping.

This change record covers the **documentation refactor only**: the three dossiers are consolidated
into `05-execution-core.md` (each old dossier reduced to a dated SUPERSEDED stub, then deleted
2026-08-18 per user request; full historical content remains in git at `74f3d89`). The audit
tooling, index, coverage table, decision index, and service READMEs are updated so the doc gates
stay green. The upstream build contracts
(`docs/04_contracts/05-babysitter.md`/`06-action-capture.md`/`07-executor.md`), the executor
requirements, and DEC-006 are **not** re-scoped in this change — that re-scope is the recorded
gate before implementation (see `05-execution-core.md` §Open gates and upstream impact). Live
money stays BLOCKED; no broker call path changed or was enabled.
