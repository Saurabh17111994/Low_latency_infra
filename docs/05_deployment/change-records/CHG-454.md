## Why

- The **mid-spike class**: background changelog→RocksDB materializations cost 91–216 ms
  per event with 0.2–6.3 MB/subtask state at **1–2 staggered rounds per subtask per
  900 s** at the 10 min default (design-note facts, CT-4A trail).
- In a 900 s capture window the 30 min interval removes in-window materialization rounds
  entirely (the first would fire at t+30 min, past run end).
- **Recorded trade:** a larger single materialization later and a longer restore replay;
  production adoption needs the restore drill in both directions (design-note guardrail).
