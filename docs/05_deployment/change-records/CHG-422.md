# CHG-422 — L3-3: the idle-watchdog state is per subtask, not per construction

- **Status:** complete — the episode latch and the job-wide last-event clock live in a shared `SourceIdleWatchdogState` held by a serializable supplier; a second generator on one subtask inherits them instead of resetting them.
- **Filed:** 2026-09-29
- **Source:** L3-3 (Compute L) from `docs/plans/2026-09-28-low-findings-remediation.md`.

## Notes

- **Why the old test pinned the bug:** `constructorRearmsEpisodeLatch` asserted that constructing a new
  generator cleared the latch. That was P2-169's intent for a *fresh job* in a reused JVM, but the
  constructor is also hit by split reassignment on a live subtask — where re-arming hides idleness.
  The two meanings are now separate: a fresh state (new job/supplier) re-arms; a second generator on
  the same subtask does not.
- **The clock half matters as much as the latch:** the job-wide clock drives the idle-at-tail alert
  (P2-055). Restamping it at a second construction made the alert wait another full `SOURCE_IDLE_ALERT_MS`
  even though the job had been quiet for the elapsed time.
- **Serializability is load-bearing:** the supplier travels inside the serialized `WatermarkStrategy`.
  The supplier-carrying constructor is clock-free on purpose — a captured `System::currentTimeMillis`
  method reference is not serializable, which would fail job submission. The new serialization test
  pins the contract.
