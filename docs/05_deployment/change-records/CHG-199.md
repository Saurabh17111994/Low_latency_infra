# CHG-199 — python-suite gate budget 300s raised to 600s (budget fit quiet runs, not gate load)

- **Status:** recorded — fixed and guarded (2026-09-17)
- **Filed:** 2026-09-17
- **Source:** the `make gate` re-run after CHG-198: step [3/19] failed with the
  suite timeout-killed mid-run.

## What happened

Step [3/19] (`timeout -k 60 300 python3 -m unittest discover`) killed the
suite about a third of the way through (`test_i*` files, log ends mid-dots,
no summary). The log contains **zero** `FAIL`/`ERROR` lines — no test failed;
the budget ran out. The 300 s budget was measured against a quiet tree
(294 s), but the gate runs the suite while the full stack is up (Flink,
Fluss, OpenObserve on CPU), where the same suite runs much slower. The budget
must cover the conditions the gate actually runs in — this is the fallback
the wave-36 work pre-agreed ("raise only if the budget flakes under load").

## What changed

- `code/01_platform/04_scripts/run-monday-gates.sh` — step 3
  `PY_TIMEOUT_SEC="${PY_TIMEOUT_SEC:-300}"` → `:-600}`, with a comment citing
  this record. Still env-overridable; `-k 60` kill-after unchanged.
- `code/01_platform/04_scripts/tests/test_gate_runner_wave21.py` — the
  `("PY_TIMEOUT_SEC", "300")` pin → `"600"`, with a comment citing this
  record. Deliberately **not** relaxed: the suite must stay env-overridable
  with a stated default, and every other timeout pin is untouched.
