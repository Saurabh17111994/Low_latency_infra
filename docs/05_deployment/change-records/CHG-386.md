## Notes

- **Why the conjunct was wrong:** CHG-356 measured a 22 min 56 s replay at a cold start; an ack can
  land before the tablet finishes replaying, so "ever acked" is not "metadata stable".
- **Still bounded and fail-closed:** the retry lives only inside
  `INGESTION_WRITE_STARTUP_GRACE_MS`; expiry and every non-metadata class keep the R-285 FATAL.
- **No new mode:** `0` still disables the grace exactly as before.
