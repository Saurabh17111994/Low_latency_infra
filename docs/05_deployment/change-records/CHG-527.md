## What changed

- `tail(CapturingAppender)`: `List<String> messages = List.copyOf(capture.messages);`
  before computing the last-40 slice. `List.copyOf` takes an atomic snapshot;
  the previous `capture.messages.subList(...)` iterated a
  `CopyOnWriteArrayList$COWSubList`, whose iterator throws when the backing
  list grows between `size()` and `String.join`.

## Why

- The bridge stderr-drain thread appends to the same list while the test
  formats its failure-context message; the race is timing-dependent and only
  manifested under the gate's parallel step load (never in plain module runs).
