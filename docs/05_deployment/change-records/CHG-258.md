# CHG-258 — the alert path retries forever, because it is infrastructure

## Why

Every service in the stack inherits one of two restart policies, and the choice was never written
down or tested. The audit found one service on the wrong side of it:

```text
$ docker service inspect prod_alert-consumer --format '{{json .Spec.TaskTemplate.RestartPolicy}}'
{"Condition":"any","Delay":5000000000,"MaxAttempts":4,"Window":120000000000}
```

`alert-consumer` merges `*deploy_workload` (the trading-services anchor), so it stops after four
restarts in 120 seconds. Its job is to receive every alert the platform emits and forward it to the
webhook; if the webhook target is down for more than two minutes, the alerts stop being consumed
until an operator notices `0/1` and re-runs `docker service update --force`. For a service that
exists to make failures visible, disappearing quietly is the one behaviour it must not have.

## What changed

- **`code/01_platform/01_docker/docker-stack.yml`** — `alert-consumer` overrides the inherited
  `restart_policy` with `condition: any` and no `max_attempts`, matching `openobserve`,
  `otel-collector`, the two new agents and the three ZooKeeper services. The comment records why the
  inherited budget does not fit.
- **`code/01_platform/04_scripts/tests/test_09_stack.py`** — `TestRestartBudget`:
  `test_every_service_is_classified` requires each of the 18 services to appear in a `BOUNDED` or
  `UNBOUNDED` set (a new service must state its intent), and the two following tests assert that the
  eight trading services carry `max_attempts` + `window` while the ten infrastructure and executor
  services do not. The sets are the documented decision:

| Class | Services | Policy | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trading / stateful | `fluss-coordinator`, `fluss-tablet-1..3`, `flink-jobmanager`, `flink-taskmanager`, `ingestion`, `execution-bridge` | bounded, 4 per 120 s | a service that cannot start after a fixed error — a missing DDL catalog, a rejected credential — must stop churning and wait for an operator, visibly, at `0/1` |
| Infrastructure | `zookeeper-1..3`, `otel-collector`, `openobserve`, `alert-consumer`, `node-exporter`, `cadvisor` | unbounded | a stopped ZooKeeper is a lost quorum, a stopped collector or agent is a hole in the single pane, a stopped alert consumer drops alerts — none of them announces itself |
| Executors | `execution-gateway`, `nautilus` | unbounded | an executor is fail-closed by design: it stays halted and must come back on its own when its dependency returns or the environment is fixed |

## Note

The bounded policy's effect was already observed end to end on this cluster, from the other side:
`ingestion` spent its budget after the bridge crashed (`0/1`, restart budget `max_attempts: 4,
window: 120s`) and stayed down instead of churning. The policy does what this record claims it does;
this change moves one service from that class to the other.
