# CHG-226 — a scenario must outlast the apply tool's drain budget

- **Status:** recorded — fixed (2026-09-18)
- **Filed:** 2026-09-18
- **Source:** the live smoke run that verified CHG-224 (`logs/ddl-bench/smoke/timestamped.log`).

## What happened

Measured on 2026-09-18, on a cluster that was healthy but carrying a teardown backlog:

| scenario | result | wall |
|---|---|---|
| 1 `[skip-smoke]` | PASS (exit 0) | 22.4 s |
| 2 `[no-ack]` (smoke on) | PASS (exit 0) | **882 s of its 900 s budget** |
| 4 `[container bad-ownership]` | **FAIL: timed out after 900 s** | 900 s |

`DdlApplyTool.DRAIN_BUDGET` is **25 minutes** (1500 s): an apply is *entitled* to wait that long for
its own scratch teardown before it reports. The smoke's per-scenario timeout was 900 s, so a
healthy-but-slow cluster made scenario 4 fail from the outside — and with it the smoke, and with it
gate step 11. That is a false negative in the certificate: nothing was wrong except that the outer
wait was shorter than the inner one. Scenario 2 finishing with 18 s to spare shows how close this
sits to a red gate on any given run.

## The change

`ddl_apply_smoke.py`: `SCENARIO_TIMEOUT_S` 900 → **1800** (30 min = the tool's 25 min plus headroom,
and equal to the gate's own `DDL_SMOKE_TIMEOUT_SEC` default), with the coupling written down next to
the constant.

This only ever bounds the *outer* wait, so it cannot mask a failure: a scenario that genuinely
deviates still fails, and later. The worst case for step 11 grows (3 × 1800 s) only on a cluster
that is pathologically slow; the measured healthy case is the 17 m 16 s of run 6.

## Verification

- New coupling leg in `ScenarioBudgetTest`, test-first: red with
  `AssertionError: 900 not greater than 1500 : a scenario may only wait SCENARIO_TIMEOUT_S=900s
  while the apply tool is entitled to wait DRAIN_BUDGET=1500s for its own teardown — the scenario
  would time out on a slow-but-healthy cluster and fail the gate`, then green after the change.
- `Ran 9 tests … OK` for `test_ddl_apply_smoke.py`; the constants now read
  `SCENARIO_TIMEOUT_S = 1800` (smoke) against `DRAIN_BUDGET = Duration.ofMinutes(25)` (tool), and the
  test fails if either moves alone.
