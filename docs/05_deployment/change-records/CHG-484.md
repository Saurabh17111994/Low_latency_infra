## Notes

- The 15 s stored forming row per tick is deliberate: the operator's
  requirement is the in-memory strategy view; what the table stores remains the
  separate storage decision (the finding (b) options). This change does not
  alter Fluss growth.
- Strategies must now tolerate a forming row of any timeframe in `onLiveTick`;
  the row's `TF` field tells them which candle is evolving. The canonical
  (FIFTEEN_S) row is always emitted first in a tick.
- `compute.candles.live.tick.emitted` stays per accepted tick (not per row).
- **ANNOTATED 2026-10-01 (CHG-486):** the stored forming row described in the
  scope is superseded — storage is closed-only (one sealed row per window at
  close; nothing at the live cadence). The all-TF in-memory feed, the
  canonical-row KPI and the C2 pending-snapshot pinning are unchanged.
