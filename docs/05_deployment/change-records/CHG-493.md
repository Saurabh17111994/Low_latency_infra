## Why (verified in the tree)

- `day_run.py:56-59` reads the manifests from `ROOT.parent / "Arrow_broker" /
  "instruments" / "cash_stocks"` — outside the repo. The guide's §2.2 rsync
  transferred only `./`, so `make day ARGS="start"` would refuse with
  "manifest has no instrument rows".
- `.gitignore:2` (`target/`) and `:6` (`*.jar`): `compute.jar` is never
  tracked, and the compute image deliberately does not bake it
  (`02_compute/Dockerfile` — "The compute.jar is NOT copied here — it is mounted
  by compose"), mounted at `docker-compose.yml:1246`; the Flink/Fluss plugin
  jars are mounted at `docker-compose.yml:399-843`.
- The VM has no JDK/Maven (the `.env.vm` `EOD_RUNNER=compose` rationale), so
  these artifacts must be transferred, not built on the VM.
