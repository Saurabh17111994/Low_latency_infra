## What changed

1. **`EodControllerTool tiering [--list] [--set on|off] [--tables <name>]`**:
   - `--list` (the default): prints every live table as
     `tiering <name>=enabled|disabled`, or `=unreadable` on a read failure
     (exit 1 -- fail-closed for the S3 guard), plus a
     `RESULT=... TABLES=... ENABLED=...` summary line.
   - `--set on|off`: one `Admin.alterTable` of `table.datalake.enabled` on
     exactly one table; a refusal (legacy table / stale lake table) is reported
     with the recreate / move-aside guidance and exits 1.
   - `--list` and `--set` are mutually exclusive at parse level.
2. **`r2_archive_sync.py`**: reads the list (`--tables` or env `EOD_TABLES`),
   lists live flags through the pinned launcher, plans with
   `r2_archive_selection.plan()` (the single rule source, shared with S3),
   prints the plan; **dry-run by default**; `--apply` executes the ALTERs one
   table at a time and exits 1 on any failure, 2 on config/listing errors
   (empty list, missing table, unreadable flag, failed listing).
