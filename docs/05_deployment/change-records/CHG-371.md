# CHG-371 — M1-5: action-scoped durable attempt identity (place/modify/cancel)

- **Status:** complete — the durable attempt claim identity is now
  `(instruction_id, action, request_hash)` with `action = cmd_env.command`. A place, its later
  modify, and its cancel share the instruction but are distinct money movements: each claims its
  own stream, a same-action replay is a `Duplicate`, a same-action changed hash is a
  `ContractViolation` halt, and a different action is always a fresh claim. Before this, an amend
  or cancel reusing its place's `instruction_id` with a different payload was misread as a
  contract violation and halted the gate when durable attempts were on — the precondition H1-3's
  flag flip was blocked on.

## Notes

- **Journal default direction:** an absent `action` decodes as `"place"` (not "unknown"): the
  conservative reading of an unlabeled pre-M1-5 record is the stream that existed then, so a
  replayed place stays a duplicate and a changed place hash stays a violation. H1-3's flag flip
  still owes a live drill before any real journal matters.
- **Offline protocol parity:** `Command.action` was added so the offline `ExecutionGate` exercises
  the same identity the live leg uses, rather than passing a constant.
- **`has_duplicate`/`has_instruction` are now action-scoped too** — they are the trait's stated key
  layout, and leaving them instruction-wide would have documented the wrong contract even though
  production classifies through `try_claim`.
