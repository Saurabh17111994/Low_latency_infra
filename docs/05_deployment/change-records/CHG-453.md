## Why

CHG-452's attribution: the TaskManager shows 256 GC/safepoint events ≥ 15 ms (max 48.6 ms)
in 900 s on a fixed 2.15 GiB heap with G1's **default `MaxGCPauseMillis=200`** — 40–50 ms
pauses are exactly what that default permits, and a 43.3 ms pause sits 2 s before the
12:09:05 tick spike. Design-note guardrails for W3-a: rows/s flat (≈ 4 907), pause counters
improve, no heap growth.

## What changed

- `code/01_platform/01_docker/docker-compose.yml`, service `flink-taskmanager`:
  `-XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=20` appended to the `FLINK_ENV_JAVA_OPTS` folded scalar (no inline
  comments there — they would become part of the value).
- Applied by recreate (`docker compose up -d flink-taskmanager`, 2026-09-30), not restart:
  live cmdline verified to carry
  `-Xmx2308544916 -XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=20 -XX:StartFlightRecording=…`.
- Static pin `test_tm_g1_pause_target_is_pinned` (gate step 3) so a compose edit cannot
  silently revert the tuning.
