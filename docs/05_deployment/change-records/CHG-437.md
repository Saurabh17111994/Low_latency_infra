## Notes

- **Why the pin is not derived:** per the constant's own doctrine (docs_audit C1), only an independent literal notices a table dropped from both the manifest and the ddl/ enumeration at once. Deriving it from either would make the two impossible to disagree and remove that detection; the guard adds a fast failure without removing the independence.
- **The other two copies are self-checking:** docs_audit's C1 pin is checked against the manifest and its C9 pin against the enumeration in the same step (they also cross-check manifest↔ddl agreement), which is why they passed while the smoke's copy failed.
