## What changed

`code/01_platform/06_stage_profiler/stage-profile.sh`:

1. New `fleet_only_capture()` — starts the docker/PSI stats sampler and the 2 s
   disk-latency probe (`io-latency-probe.sh`), writes `stages/run-meta.txt`
   (`mode=fleet-only`, capture seconds, rate, container count, OTLP mirror
   path), waits out the capture window, stops the probe and tears the fleet
   down.
2. `run_phase()` — with `SKIP_JOB=1`, the phase runs `compile_probes` +
   `warmup` (both need no job: the warm-up signal is the raw log advancing, read
   through `FlussReadLagProbe`) and then the fleet-only capture, instead of
   `submit_job`/`start_cp_phase_sampler`/`run_capture`/`sample_raw`. Those legs
   stay skipped because without a job they are meaningless: no vertex map, no
   Flink KPI snapshots, no checkpoint phases, empty probe reads.
3. Entry point — with `SKIP_JOB=1` the smoke phase ends the run (exit 0) instead
   of running the presence gate and the report, which read the job and would be
   empty by construction.

What still runs, and is the point of the mode: the per-ingestion
`docker logs -f` mirrors opened by `start_fleet`
(`capture/j1-N/java.out` — the client-side OTLP `append.latency.ms`
p50/p90/p99 series at its hardcoded 10 s cadence), the docker/PSI stats
sampler, and the io-latency TSV. The tablet-side poller is supplied by the
caller (it has to run inside the tablet container), which keeps this hunk
small and out of the timed window.

## Not changed

- `BRINGUP_ONLY` keeps its meaning (bring-up proof, then tear down).
- `run_capture`/`stage-capture.sh` are untouched: in fleet-only mode the frame
  is not used at all instead of being taught to run without a job.
- No new dependency, no new `make gate` step, no deploy artifact.
